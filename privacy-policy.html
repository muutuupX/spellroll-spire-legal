Privacy Policy

Spellroll Spire · Effective date: 18 November 2026

Spellroll Spire ("the game") is made by Pradit Kanchai ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game does not sell or share your personal information, and the only data that leaves your device is the record of purchases you make.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your own device, so you can keep playing:

  • Game progress: heroes, stats, cards, companions, credits and the run in progress
  • Settings: volume levels, graphics mode and story options
  • A record of the purchases you made, so they stay unlocked

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or contact details
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking or third-party advertising

The game works fully offline. It goes online only to make a purchase or to check which purchases you own.

In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see or store your payment details.

To confirm and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service that acts on our behalf. When you buy something or restore purchases, RevenueCat receives:

  • the store's purchase receipt (which products were bought, when, and in which country's store)
  • a random, anonymous ID that the game creates on your device; it is not linked to your name, email or store account details

This is used only to check that a purchase is genuine and to unlock it again when you reinstall the game while signed in to the same store account. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. To have this purchase record deleted, email us at the address below.

  • RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts using another service that collects information (for example cloud saves), we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released.
